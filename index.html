01 / About

How I approach research

I am Shiyu Liu, a third-year undergraduate at the School of Information, Renmin University of China, pursuing dual degrees in Finance and Data Science & Big Data Technology. My research centers on large language models and how they represent preferences, interact with incentives, and make decisions under uncertainty.

Our ACL 2026 paper studies stable matching under probabilistic preferences. It brings together LLM-based preference modeling and algorithmic game theory, and has led me to broader questions about LLM agents: how to evaluate their behavior, make their decisions more reliable, and understand their role in interactive systems.

I hope to pursue a PhD in computer science outside mainland China. I want to combine clear theoretical questions with careful experiments and develop methods that make LLM-based decision systems more dependable in practice.

ACL 2026 · Accepted Accepted conference paper

Modeling and Solving Stable Matching under Probabilistic Preferences with Large Language Models

Yuqi Kong*, Shiyu Liu*, Jiaxu Li, Hongtao Liu, Qi Qi, Weiran Shen * Equal contribution

We study stable matching when preferences are uncertain. The paper introduces Expected Blocking Pairs to measure instability and proposes a hybrid Gale-Shapley-LLM approach.
